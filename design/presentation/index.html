Understanding

It hears you,
even when you mumble.

Voice recognition splits and slurs words constantly. Voicy's parser glues them back together — across three tokens and twelve trigger variants.

You said
"Write Chine hi"
↓
Chine → "hi"
ASR slurred it
"writechinehi"
↓
Chine → "hi"
Split across tokens
"write chi ne hi"
↓
Chine → "hi"
Wrong trigger
"send tim how are you"
↓
Timofey → "how are you"

AI assistant

Ask anything.
Get answered.

Say "give answer" before your question. Voicy routes it through Gemini for speed, or a local 400 MB LLM if you'd rather stay offline.

you Give answer — what's the capital of Mongolia?
ai Ulaanbaatar. Coldest capital city in the world.
you Give answer — convert 200 fahrenheit to celsius
ai About 93.3°C. Just below boiling water.
